Gate 15

Next we encounter the harmonious Gate 15. It can be called a universal Gate, since it is present in every living form. Through its basic inner quality, Gate 15 unites the entire planet, including plants, and this quality is being at rest. If your life rhythms swing between extremes, it is very important that there be a certain inner calm at the same time. It is a natural point between the extremes, and it is from this state that one can move toward one's extremes in either direction.

The text of Hexagram 15 speaks of a balance between extremes. The lower trigram is stillness, the mountain. The upper one is receptivity, the earth. The ancient Chinese had another word to describe this quality, one that I prefer: a room, or space. In order to be receptive, we need a space in which to receive what comes from outside. When doubled, this trigram forms Hexagram 2 of Receptivity, which stands in opposition to Hexagram 1, responsible for chi. This connection demonstrates our capacity to receive the general life energy of chi and express it as our personal chi.
