Gate 30

Harmonious to Gate 41 is Gate 30, called Clinging, or Clinging Fire. This is a property of fire that seems to stick to material, clinging to its exposed parts. If you set a sheet of paper on fire, this quality of fire is demonstrated very clearly. From the perspective of the digestive system, Gate 30 determines the volume of the incoming mass and, in addition, ensures the movement of food through the intestine.

Both trigrams of Hexagram 30 represent Clinging Fire. There are other ancient Chinese names as well: burning, light. This is the burning of life—here we feel this hot energy in the process of digestion and sense that life is happening not only outside, but inside as well. I have already said that the emotional system is directly connected with the nervous system, without which we would neither feel anything nor understand how much food we need.
