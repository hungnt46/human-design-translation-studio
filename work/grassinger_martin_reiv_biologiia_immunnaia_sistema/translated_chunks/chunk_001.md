Introduction

Most of you are familiar with how the centres of the BodyGraph correspond to particular parts of the hormonal system. For example, the Root Centre is connected with the adrenal glands, and the Throat with the thyroid system... But there are many systems within our body: the digestive, nervous, lymphatic, cardiovascular and immune systems... Depending on which specific system we are looking at, certain centres will be involved in it, each in its own way. And if, for example, we consider the digestive system, the way the centres function will be entirely different from their functioning in relation to the immune system.

So... What is health? How can it be formally defined? The World Health Organization has a definition, which reads: “Health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being.” In other words, it is not the absence of illness or some disorder; it is a state. It consists of three areas: the state of the body, the state of the mind, and the emotional state. This means that if you are filled with mental fears, that will be unhealthy for the body as well. If you are isolated from society and have no social contacts, that too will be unhealthy for your organism. And if the physical body suffers, this is reflected, one way or another, in both the mental and emotional sides of life. Everything is interconnected.

What was most extraordinary to me was that everything could be found in these 64 hexagrams. Human Design was the first system in which I began to find precise answers to the most difficult questions. In this course, I use a great deal of information that the Voice transmitted to us through Ra.
