# Revise Vietnamese Naturalness

Revise the Vietnamese translation to eliminate translationese.

This is NOT a free rewrite.

The source remains the semantic authority.

Preserve:
- all substantive meaning;
- approved general and relevant domain terminology;
- tone;
- modality;
- uncertainty;
- conditions;
- examples;
- rhetorical intent.

You may restructure Vietnamese syntax when necessary.

Pay particular attention to:
- literal source-language clause order;
- excessive `của`;
- unnecessary `một`;
- mechanical `được` / `bị`;
- unnecessary repeated pronouns;
- nominalization;
- literal collocations;
- unnatural connectors;
- excessively formal vocabulary;
- sentences that are grammatically correct but clearly sound translated.

Do not:
- summarize;
- simplify;
- embellish;
- explain;
- make the text more literary;
- add information.

After revision, compare the result silently against the source to verify that no meaning drift occurred.

Output only the revised Vietnamese translation.
