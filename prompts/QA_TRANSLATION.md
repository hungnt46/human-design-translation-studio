# Translation Audit

Compare the complete source and complete translation.

Do not rewrite the translation unless explicitly requested.

Audit for:

1. omitted source content;
2. added meaning;
3. mistranslation;
4. incorrect negation;
5. changed certainty or modality;
6. changed causal relationships;
7. general or relevant domain terminology errors;
8. inconsistent terminology;
9. names, numbers, identifiers, units, formulas, code, technical references, and dates;
10. structural omissions;
11. duplicated content;
12. chunk-boundary discontinuities;
13. translationese;
14. unnatural target-language phrasing.

For every genuine issue provide:
- location;
- source;
- current translation;
- problem;
- recommended correction;
- severity: Critical / Major / Minor.

Do not invent issues merely to populate the report.

Write the audit to `qa/`.
