# Human Design Domain Glossary

This optional domain glossary applies only when the source is about Human Design or the user explicitly requests it. It is not a repository-wide default.

## Status rules

- **APPROVED** — must be used exactly as specified.
- **PRESERVE** — keep the source term unchanged.
- **PENDING** — not canonical; do not treat as approved.

## Rules

1. APPROVED terminology overrides stylistic variation.
2. Do not replace an approved term with a synonym merely to avoid repetition.
3. A fixed term constrains vocabulary, not the grammar of the surrounding sentence.
4. If the source contains an important Human Design term not listed here and a translation could distort its meaning, preserve the source term where appropriate and flag it for review.
5. Do not silently add new APPROVED terms.
