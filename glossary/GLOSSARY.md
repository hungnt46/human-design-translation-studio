# General Glossary

This file contains canonical terminology that applies across subjects in this repository.

Use a separate domain glossary when terminology belongs only to one field. For example, `HD_GLOSSARY.md` applies only to Human Design material. Other domain glossaries may be added as `<DOMAIN>_GLOSSARY.md`.

## Status rules

- **APPROVED** — must be used exactly as specified for the stated language pair and scope.
- **PRESERVE** — keep the source term unchanged.
- **PENDING** — not canonical; do not treat as approved.

## Rules

1. The user's explicit instruction takes precedence over this glossary.
2. APPROVED terminology overrides stylistic variation within its stated language pair and scope.
3. Do not replace an approved term with a synonym merely to avoid repetition.
4. A fixed term constrains vocabulary, not the grammar of the surrounding sentence.
5. Do not silently add new APPROVED terms.
6. Put subject-specific terms in a domain glossary rather than making them global.
